# PALACE — RESULTS SUMMARY (fast mode; all numbers trace to results/paper_numbers.json and results/tables/*.csv)
**Run mode:** `fast` everywhere (one representative week per season, Surat 2019 weather, test-split occupancy replayed; 6 office sites; controller seeds 0–2; DP forecaster seeds 0–2 except eps_train=0.5 = seed 0 only; non-DP forecasters 5 seeds). No full-year runs. SYNTHETIC components: lighting preferences, personal thermal offsets, CCT schedules, the CASAS-site -> occupant link, weather/occupancy year pairing.

## 2. Forecaster (T3, F4; 6 test sites aggregated over office clients with >=10 test days)
AUROC: B2 central 0.898 (CI 0.877–0.917, 5 seeds) ≈ B3 local 0.898 ≈ B4 FedAvg 0.888; naive baselines: persistence 0.773, weekday-profile 0.829, logistic regression 0.877. DP-FedAvg (B5): 0.876 (eps=8), 0.871 (eps=2), 0.856 (eps=0.5, 1 seed). DP-FedPer (PALACE): 0.858 (eps=8), 0.809 (eps=2), **0.691 (eps=0.5, 1 seed)** — personalised local heads degrade much faster under DP than the shared head. **Negative findings:** (i) federation gives no accuracy gain over local training at these data volumes; (ii) DP-FedPer is worse than DP-FedAvg at every eps; (iii) next-day arrival-time MAE is poor (140–300 min). Training clip norm C=0.25 was chosen on validation AUROC. A causality leak (GroupNorm over time) was found and fixed before any reported result (it had inflated AUROC by ~0.04).

## 3. Privacy statements (exact)
- **Training privacy:** day-level (record-level) (eps, delta)-DP of each client's released DP-SGD updates under the RDP accountant, delta = 1/(10·N_train_days) per client, cumulative eps over all rounds on one persistent accountant per client (spent eps matches target, e.g. 0.99 for target 1 in the smoke run). **Not client-level DP** (10 clients). A record's 48 h input context overlaps the next two records, so protection of one day's raw data is up to 3x weaker (group privacy). Post-hoc temperature scaling, the F1 threshold and C selection use validation days and are NOT in the accounted budget. Secure aggregation not simulated.
- **Actuation privacy (APF):** exponential mechanism over all 1,089 first actions; eps_act-DP w.r.t. the presence bit at that step (event level), conditional on state and forecast; sensitivity Delta_u = w_c/(H·Σw) verified numerically (tests/test_control.py). Over T steps basic composition gives T·eps_act (zCDP/advanced bounds in apf.composition). **Not covered:** whole-trajectory guarantees against side information; leakage through the physical state and the electricity meter.
- **Empirical audits (not proofs):** loss-threshold MIA on final models: AUC 0.51–0.55 for all methods/eps (central 0.526, local 0.550, DP-FedAvg 0.52–0.53, DP-FedPer 0.515–0.531), TPR@1%FPR 0.02–0.05; LiRA-lite (8 shadows, single-site DP proxy, 2 sites): AUC 0.49–0.54. **The attacks are near chance everywhere, so they cannot discriminate between eps levels (no eps trend observed); this is a null result, not evidence of strong privacy.**
- **Actuation adversary** (GBT; trained on 7-day×3-season validation replays — a strong assumption): B1 presence AUROC 1.00; PALACE no-APF 0.999; APF eps_act=0.5/2/8: **actuation-only 0.77/0.77/0.83** (leakage L 0.54/0.54/0.65) but **actuation+meter 0.996** and **meter-only 0.98–0.99**. The meter trace (incl. plug-load step 10 vs 2.5 W/m² when occupied, a simulator ASSUMPTION that makes this an upper bound) defeats the APF entirely.
- **APF cost:** at eps_act=0.5/2/8 the filter raises lighting+HVAC energy by 111–143 % vs B1 and cuts comfort to 0.60–0.62, because the first-action utility spread (~0.15) is small relative to Delta_u (~0.04): meaningful privacy requires near-uniform random actions. Pareto set (carbon, discomfort, leakage) has 4 non-dominated configurations (pareto.csv).

## 4. Granularity ladder (reduced; F7; central non-DP forecaster, 3 seeds)
R1 raw features AUROC 0.897 / MIA 0.510; R2 aggregate counts 0.896 / 0.506; R3 DP-noised counts (per-bin Laplace+RR, NOT a day-level guarantee) eps=4: 0.896 / 0.510, eps=1: 0.886 / 0.520. The "individual" regime (Aruba, the only verified single-resident home) was NOT run through the controller.

## 5. Sustainability (F11, T6; estimates)
Training energy (CodeCarbon offline, machine-level estimate on an i5-12450H + RTX 3050): ≈0.003–0.004 kWh per DP-FL run (≈200 s); communication ≈31 MB per run (energy assumed 0.06 kWh/GB, UNVERIFIED). PALACE without APF saves 0.55 kWh per 25 m² zone-day vs B1 (CI 0.48–0.61) = ≈145 kg CO2e per zone-year (band 89–210, using grid factor 0.727 kg/kWh, **unverified secondary source**, ±30 %). Payback is hours, but this counts only the final training runs, not development, sweeps or simulation energy, and the saving is from a reduced-order simulator, not a measurement. The pre-registered default (APF on, eps_act=8) is net NEGATIVE: −2.63 kWh per zone-day (−697 kg CO2e/zone-year). Scale-up to many buildings is NOT computed (would be extrapolation).

## 6. Simulator validation
2R2C + cooling-only AC + LED model with 9 physics tests passing; B0/B1 annual electricity 70–144 kWh/m² lies inside BDG2's office 10th–90th percentile (53–293) with no tuning — a plausibility check only (BDG2 offices are mostly non-tropical; plug load dominates). Robustness ±30 % (F12): see paper_numbers `ctrl.PALACE_sim_*`; the +30 % unfavourable case raises energy and discomfort (T4).

## 7. Limitations
Synthetic lighting preferences and thermal offsets; synthetic CASAS-to-ASHRAE persona link; ASHRAE-trained thermal model (US/Europe-heavy; p(no change) decreases monotonically over 22–32 °C, driving setpoints low); reduced-order simulator with perfect state estimation; PIR presence is high/noisy at 24 h-active sites and most office sites have one sensor; only 5 usable office sites + stairwells, museum has ~no test days; occupancy and weather come from different years; one week per season; 3 seeds (DP/controller); hidden synthetic occupant is one per zone; single climate; no field deployment; grid factor unverified; actuation adversary strong; per-step APF only.

## 8. Everything that failed or deviated
Python 3.13 instead of 3.10–3.11; GPU 3.7 GiB total; ASHRAE licence not stated in repo (needs Dryad check); CEA grid-factor primary PDF inaccessible; encrypted-HVAC reference (arXiv:2312.05183) unverified and omitted; CCT grid is 380 K steps (spec arithmetic inconsistent); controller weights frozen a priori (no validation sweep); default eps_act changed 2->8 before the grid (PREREGISTERED amendment); GroupNorm causality bug fixed; seeds cut to 3 (eps 0.5: 1); cut entirely: second climate, PPO B6, clients 3/6/9 ablation, horizon ablation, R4, dwell/top-K APF add-ons, full-year runs, S1–S4, leave-one-site-out, misspecification of controller prefs (only RR-feedback misspecification in results/tables/feedback_rr_summary.csv). Home sites (Aruba etc.) were not used in the controller grid and no DP-FL home run was executed.
